Our philosophy

A good puzzle is worth
a thousand features.

No subscriptions. No energy bars. No guilt trips. Just you and a puzzle that's genuinely worth your time.

Focus

Every screen does one thing. No dark patterns, no notification traps, no endless loops designed to steal another hour. You play when you want to — and stop just as easily.

Timeless

Logic puzzles have been captivating minds for centuries. We're not chasing trends or manufacturing seasons. We build games people will still want to play years from now.
